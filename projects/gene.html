🔭 Genomic Investigation

This project explores high-dimensional human gene expression data to identify statistically significant and reproducible molecular patterns separating glioma and non-tumor brain tissue. The framework combines differential expression analysis, dimensionality reduction, machine learning classification and stability selection.
